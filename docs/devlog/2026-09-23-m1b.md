### `4356f29` + `3178fa1` — owner feedback #9: "too many magic → 3 FPS"
**Reproduction.** In the editor (desktop), a new world, measured with an in-game probe via godot-mcp
exec in real time:

| Scenario | Physics engine | FPS | Physics time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline, no buddies | Godot default | 59.6 | max 79 ms (terrain loading) |
| 10 house-sized buddies (308 blocks each), 5 of them glowing, spawned around the player overlapping hills | Godot default | **7.8** | max **220 ms**; one buddy fell through the world (y −53) |
| Same, buddy `_physics_process` switched off | Godot default | 45.1 | max 51.7 ms |
| 10 tiny buddies (2 blocks) | Godot default | 59.5 | max 5.3 ms |
| 1 big buddy, idle | Godot default | 58.8 | max 5.7 ms |
| 1 big buddy following the walking player | Godot default | 60.0 | max 10.6 ms |
| 1 big buddy buried in a hill | Godot default | 59.2 | max 27.4 ms (pushed out) |
| 9 big buddies dropped cleanly, idle / walking | Godot default | 39.6 / 51.8 | max 19.1 / 21.3 ms |
| 10 big buddies, same overlapping spawn as the 7.8 FPS case | **Jolt** | 52.9 | avg 18.1, max 43.8 ms |
| 10 big buddies following the walking player | Jolt | 57.0 | avg 10.0, max 11.0 ms |
| Walking, buddy physics off (baseline) | Jolt | 60.0 | avg **3.1** ms |
| 10 big buddies with the footprint capped at 1.8, walking | Jolt | 47.8 (terrain streaming) | avg **3.6** ms, max 6.4 ms |

**Root cause.** Each buddy's collision box was as wide as its whole build (for example 6.3 × 5.7 × 6.3).
Every buddy called `move_and_slide` with that box over the voxel terrain's triangle-mesh collision.
The cost grows with the box's footprint on the terrain. With Godot's default physics it explodes when a
big body overlaps the terrain (220 ms per frame, several physics ticks per frame, a spiral of death).
Phones are 3–5× slower than this CPU, which is the owner's 3 FPS.

**Fix 1 (`4356f29`), the root cause.**
- `Buddy.MAX_FOOTPRINT` = 1.8: a big buddy stands on a compact base and keeps its full height, so walls
  still stop it.
- Tests: `test_big_buddy_collides_with_a_compact_footprint` went RED→GREEN, and
  `test_small_buddy_keeps_its_own_size` was added.
- Physics with 10 big buddies walking: 10.0 → 3.6 ms average.

**Fix 2 (`3178fa1`), the engine.**
- `physics/3d/physics_engine="Jolt Physics"` (Jolt is built into Godot 4.4+ and is the default for new
  projects since 4.6).
- Test: `tests/unit/test_project_settings.gd::test_project_uses_jolt_physics` went RED→GREEN.
- Checked that walking, jumping, building and flying still behave, with the autopilot on Jolt in the
  desktop editor: **play 59.6 FPS (worst 30.6 ms), 2 km flight 59.8 FPS (worst 51.4 ms), 0 frames over
  100 ms, never stuck.**
- The autopilot now also keeps its lines in a static `results` array (readable via godot-mcp).
- 201 passing.

### Task 6: flowing water (feedback #3)
- **`core/rules/water_flow_rules.gd` (`WaterFlowRules`, pure):**
  - Water falls first. On a floor it spreads up to 5 blocks, and falling keeps the distance, so every
    flood stays within reach of its source.
  - Water never spreads onto the top of other water, so a waterfall merges into a lake.
  - Unloaded terrain counts as closed. A step fills at most N cells, and a flood fills at most 400.
  - Flow starts when water is placed, or when a non-water block next to water is broken (water above
    or beside it). Removing water never pulls water back in, so players can clean up a puddle.
- **`world/water_flow.gd` (`WaterFlow`):** a host-only node that `WorldService.setup()` creates for
  hosts and solo play. It listens to `block_changed` and fills 12 cells every 0.15 s. Its own fills do
  not start new floods. Clients get the water through the terrain sync.
- **Bug found in-game and fixed test-first.**
  - Symptom: a river's surface water has water below it, and the first rule ("water on water merges")
    stopped it from pouring into a channel dug beside it.
  - New rule: water standing on water may pour only into places whose floor is not water.
  - Test: `test_lake_water_pours_into_a_channel_dug_beside_it` (RED, then GREEN).
- **In-game check** (godot-mcp):
  - Dug a 7-block channel from a river, 8 blocks deep.
  - The water ran 5 blocks along it ((4..8, 23, 3) became water; (10, 23, 3) stayed air), then the
    flow stopped.
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/test_water_flow_rules.gd` (14 tests) and
  `test_placed_water_flows_on_the_host` (RED with the hook disabled, then GREEN). The suite has
  236 tests, all passing.

### Task 8: realistic graphics style (feedback #2)
- **Textures:** `tools/make_realistic_atlas.py` downloads CC0 ambientCG materials, cached in
  `~/.cache/easycraft-textures`. It builds three files:
  - `data/blocks/realistic_albedo.png` and `realistic_normal.png` (2048², the same 8×8 layout as the
    pixel atlas).
  - `data/blocks/water_waves.png`.
  - Each 256 px tile is a 240 px texture plus an 8 px continuation border (wrap-around, or edge-clamp
    for grass sides and log tops), so filtering and mipmaps never bleed tiles into each other.
  - Glass, light, water, ores, crystal and log-top rings are generated.
  - Picks: Grass005, Ground103, Rock030, Ground079L, Bark014, WoodFloor043, Bricks104, Grass001
    (leaves with cut-out holes), Plastic013A (tinted toy blocks), Rock035 (deep stone), Rock058
    (bedrock, darkened), Wood095. Credits are in `assets/CREDITS.md`.
- **Imports:** VRAM-compressed with mipmaps; the normal maps are flagged as normal maps. Uncompressed,
  each atlas would take about 22 MB of video memory. The textures load only when the realistic style
  is chosen.
- **Sky:** Poly Haven "Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)", 1K HDR (1.4 MB), used as a
  `PanoramaSkyMaterial`. Glow (bloom) is on in realistic mode.
- **`BlockLibrary.set_style`:** the block materials are now created once and shared by every library,
  block mesh and living buddy. Switching swaps albedo and normal textures, filtering and roughness on
  them, so it is instant with no remeshing.
- **Water:** `shaders/water.gdshader` is used in both styles. Blocky samples the pixel tile, with the
  same sRGB fix on OpenGL. Realistic uses two scrolling world-space wave normal maps, low roughness
  and fresnel alpha. The voxel mesher already emits tangents, so normal maps work (checked).
- **Wiring:**
  - `WorldService.apply_style` handles the blocks, sky and glow. The sandbox calls it from
    `_apply_settings`, and so does the menu backdrop.
  - Settings has a new row "Style: BLOCKY / REALISTIC". The profile's `settings.style` defaults to
    `blocky`, and invalid values fall back to it (`GraphicsPreset.STYLES`).
- **In-game check** (desktop, Vulkan): realistic shows a photo sky, textured grass, dirt, bark and
  leaves, and new water. Switching back to blocky from Settings is instant, and the choice is saved.
- **Settings panel:** it now uses the full 720 px height. The next settings row needs a scroll

- **Tests:** `test_graphics_style_defaults_to_blocky`, `test_both_styles_share_the_tile_layout` and

### Task 9: animals and pet dogs (feedback #6)
- **Rules (`a02539b`):** `AnimalRules`, plus pets saved in `WorldMeta`.
  - Species: dog, cat, pig, cow, sheep, chicken, rabbit on grass, and fish in water at least 2 deep.
  - Spawning is deterministic per 32×32 area, so the same animals come back.
  - Up to 20 animals per player. Wild animals leave beyond 80 blocks; pets never do.
  - Three pets within 4 s tame a dog or cat, with a maximum of 3 pets per player.
  - Safe steps: no walking into water or off drops of more than 2 blocks, and fish stay in water.
  - Animals flee a player rushing at them.
- **Entity (`entities/animal/animal.gd`, `blocky_animal.gd`):**
  - Each species is one shared vertex-coloured box mesh, so an animal costs one draw call.
  - They hop-walk, Crossy-Road style, instead of animating separate legs; legs would cost about 6
    draws per animal.
  - The collision box footprint is at most 0.9 blocks (see feedback #9).
  - The host simulates wandering, fleeing and safe steps; fish swim.
  - Pets follow like buddies, teleport when far, sit and stand when their owner taps them, and hop
    when the owner jumps.
  - `knockback()` is there for the weapons agent. `MultiplayerSynchronizer` syncs position, rotation,
    owner, name and sitting.
