## What a child sees and does

**The Inventions book** ("How people learned to fly"): INVENT in MORE (a kite on its string), I on a PC, MORE → INVENT with
a controller (D-pad right, then Cross / A; Circle / B goes back). One paper page like the Secret Book's: a tab for each
invention in history's order with its picture and its year (long ago, 1783, the 1890s, 1903, 1939, 1957; a ✓ once
invented, pale while not yet in Survivor) and the page: the big picture, when and where, the real story, HOW IT WORKS (its
science in a box), what it opens, the School unit on its science, and the speaker that reads it all aloud. X, Esc and Back
close it (Back from the bottle rocket goes back to the page first).

| Invention | Its page (people of history only) | Made at a Workbench from (Survivor) | Gold, or free with | It opens | TRY IT (20 gold once) |

**In Survivor** nothing flies until it is invented. FLY (the button, the three quick taps, F) says "Invent a way to fly
first! INVENT (in MORE, or I on a PC) shows how people did it, from the kite on." The book opens on the next invention:
what it is made of with have / need under each material's picture (green when there is enough), its price ("10 gold, or
free once you master Pushes, pulls and friction in the School"; "Free: you mastered …!"), and MAKE IT (by a Workbench:
"Stand next to a Workbench to make it!"; the next only: "Not yet: invent The Kite next. Each invention opens the next
one!"). Made: a fanfare, "You invented the kite! …", the page says Invented! with TRY IT and FLY WITH IT. FLY then flies
the newest machine invented (or the one FLY WITH IT picked, saved in the profile); FlightLook shows it round the child in
every game: the striped balloon over a wicker basket (a flame while heating), the 1903-style biplane with its spinning
propellers, the white jet with its glow, the kite high up downwind on its string. The Mission Board says "Invent the rocket
first! …" when it opens and LAUNCH refuses until the rocket is invented.

**TRY IT** closes the book and puts the challenge in a banner under the game clock: the invention's picture, the goal, a
bar that fills and the hint for now ("Turn your back to the wind: the kite flies out in front of you."), with the wind's
arrow for the kite and the balloon. Done: its science in a line ("Up you go! Hot air is lighter than the cold air around
it, so the balloon floats.") and +20 gold the first time. The kite's try puts the kite up from the hand; the balloon's, the
plane's and the jet's take off in that machine; the glider's waits for a glide; the rocket's is the bottle rocket.

**In the Sandbox** the book is to read and try: every page's TRY IT and FLY WITH IT, and FREE FLYING back to the classic
flying, which stays as it was (owner #54 and #75: two quick taps fly). The try-its pay into the profile like the School.

**Without air** (the Moon, Europa, Io, Mercury) the kite, the balloon, the plane and the jet say "There is no air here:
kites, balloons and planes need air to fly. Only rockets fly without it!"; on Mars "Mars's air is far too thin … A tiny
helicopter did fly here in 2021, with very long, very fast blades!"; Titan's thick air flies them.

## Rulings (the owner's rule: decide and write down; cost if wrong)
1. **The book is its own panel, not a tab of the Books.** The Books' tab column already fills the 720 px screen and the
   wonders agent adds a WONDERS page there. *Cost:* a tab that opens this panel later: one button.
2. **An invention belongs to the profile** (knowledge goes into every world and is backed up: `ProfileData.inventions`,
   `ProfileBackup.merge`), its materials to the world's bag. *Cost:* per-world inventions would need `WorldMeta`.
3. **The materials are blocks the game already has** (no item or block ids were given): Sticks, Planks, Iron, Coal, Glass,
   Kerosene, and **Cloth = Sail Cloth or a sheep's wool** (the colour blocks sheep leave, `AnimalRules.WOOL`; Tilly sells
   them too), taken Sail Cloth first. *Cost:* Cloth and String items need an item id page.
4. **The price is a little gold or learning**: 10, 25, 40, 60, 100, 150 (385 in all: about a day and a half of learning,
   EarnRules' day ≈ 275), or free once the invention's School unit is mastered (one table, `InventionLine.LINE`).
   *Cost:* one number each.
5. **The jet and the rocket need kerosene** (jet fuel is kerosene; the Moon rocket's first stage burned kerosene), which
   Survivor refines from the Sandstorm Desert's oil (spec §26): the missions in Survivor need a trip to the desert.
   *Cost:* a long chain for a young child; kerosene → coal is one line each in `InventionLine.LINE`.
6. **One opens the next**, in history's order: the rocket is invented after the jet (the brief). *Cost:* none known.
7. **Survivor's paid classic flying is gone**: flying needs an invented machine, for every profile (no grandfathering).
   *Cost:* grandfathering needs a flag in the profile.
8. **Fuel**: the balloon's gas and the kite are free; the plane pays FlyCost's price (10 gold a minute × the universe's
   scale), the jet twice (`InventionRules.fuel_factor`; the host charges by the synced `flight_craft`). *Cost:* one table.
9. **The kite never flies**: FLY with the kite on high ground (the feet at least 10 blocks above the sea: wind blows
   freely up there) lifts for 1.2 s at 5 m/s (about 6 blocks), then no faster down than 2.5 m/s; on low ground it says
   "Your kite needs a windy hill". A safety line on its page. *Cost:* constants in `FlightCrafts`.
10. **The glider invented gives the glide for good; Tilly's glider stays on sale** (Lilienthal sold copies of his own
    glider: the first aircraft made to sell). *Cost:* taking it off her shelf in Survivor is one line in
    `GadgetRules.PRICES`.
11. **The plane must keep moving**: let go, it flies on at 40 % of its top speed; slower than 5 m/s in the air its wings
    stop lifting (it sinks 3 m/s, whatever UP says); on the ground it rolls, and at 8 m/s along the ground its wings lift
    it off by themselves. Speeds: plane 15 m/s, jet 20 (the classic flying is 11). *Cost:* constants; the jet's speed
    against the world's streaming on a phone is the emulator's check.
12. **The wind**: one of eight compass points from the world's seed and the time, changing every 5 minutes, 2 m/s; the
    balloon drifts with it and steers 2 m/s on top (real pilots choose a height whose wind blows their way). Another
    player's kite on this screen flies to the east (the wind is this game's). *Cost:* cosmetic.
13. **A try-it pays 20 gold once per invention** (`EarnRules.INVENT_TRY_GOLD`, on the How to earn list), in both modes; a
    LAN guest's try in a friend's world pays nothing ("Try it in your own world to earn its gold!"). *Cost:* one number.
14. **The rocket's try-it is a bottle rocket in the book** (5.5 m a pump, 8 pumps at most "as is safe", 30 m to pass),
    not a mission (the Launch Site is the real thing). *Cost:* none known.
15. **Only the Launch Site's missions are gated** (the brief): the Star Map's LAND for a planet already on the route and
    flying up to the playful planets (Boing, Rainbow) stay. *Cost:* none known.
16. **No air, no wings**: the kite, the balloon, the plane and the jet refuse on airless bodies and on Mars; Titan's and
    Venus's thick air fly them (`InventionRules.air`). The Sandbox's classic flying stays everywhere (the game's magic),
    and the glider gadget is unchanged. *Cost:* the glider's no-air rule is a next step.
17. **A LAN guest invents in its own world** (the bag here is the host's world's), like the rocket parts and prospecting.
    *Cost:* co-op inventing later.
18. **The picks**: Survivor's FLY WITH IT is saved (`InventionData.craft`), the Sandbox's is this session's; a TRY IT picks
    its machine for the session. *Cost:* none known.
19. **The banner has no HudLayout spot** (it is not movable): top centre under the game clock, display only.
    *Cost:* a spot in `HudLayout.SPOTS` if children should move it.
20. **I on a PC** (`inventions` action, the KEYS card's line); no controller button of its own (MORE → INVENT).
21. **Kept by the director**: a profile replaced mid-session (a cloud merge) keeps the old `InventionData` until the next
    world, like prospecting's. *Cost:* a minor.
22. **The MORE panel takes a fourth column past four rows** (`MorePanel.columns_for`): a phone's Sandbox in Green Meadows
    online has 13 rare buttons with INVENT, and five rows of three ran off a 720 px screen. *Cost:* a wider box (580 px)
    when it is that full.
23. **One kite lift until the feet land** (FLY pressed again in the air does nothing: the test caught a climb of 25
    blocks by pressing FLY over and over). *Cost:* none known.
