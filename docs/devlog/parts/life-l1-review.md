## Little Lifesaver

For the review by a first aid trainer (the IFRC / national Red Cross or Red Crescent, the ERC).

The device **phone** (owner #117, the device model): controls: the keys 0 to 9; the speaker button. It shows: the numbers pressed; CALLING.... The right order: pick it up → press 112 → say what happened and where you are → answer the questions → stay on the line until they say goodbye. Safety: only for a real emergency: never to try it; an address is said out loud, never typed into a game.

### Little (about 6-8): Call for help

The Expert try's twist: The helper on the phone asks one more question: which helpers you need.

Steps:

- **hello**: words, then OK
  - Coach: "Oh no! Bobo the buddy slipped and fell. Let's practise how to help. This is practice. A real first aid course teaches more."
- **safe**: pick a picture

  - Picture (cross) not this one: "Not safe": "Look again: nothing here can hurt you. It is safe to help."
  - After: "You looked first. Checking it is safe keeps you safe too."
- **ask**: touch it `buddy_shoulder` (motion press)
  - Coach: "Touch Bobo's shoulder and ask: are you all right?"
  - After: "Bobo says: ouch, my leg! Bobo is awake and talking."
- **phone**: touch it `phone` (device phone)
  - Coach: "Now we call for help. Pick up the phone."
  - After: "Good. Now press the numbers."
- **dial**: press in order `the keys of 112` (motion press, device phone)
  - Coach: "Press 112: one one two."
  - After: "You called 112. A calm helper answers."
- **what**: pick a picture
  - Coach: "The helper asks: what happened?"
  - Picture (buddy_hurt) right: "My friend fell and hurt a leg"
  - Picture (toy) not this one: "I lost my toy"
  - Picture (food) not this one: "I'm hungry"
  - A miss: "Tell the helper what is wrong, so they send the right help."
  - After: "Yes: you said what happened."
- **where**: pick a picture

  - Picture (house) right: "My address (I learn it with my grown-up)"

- **end**: words, then OK

3. Push hard and fast in the middle of the chest, 100 to 120 a minute.

The "name it" card: **Profit, interest and scams**

1. Profit = what comes in - what it costs.
2. Compound interest grows on the interest too.
3. Plan a budget: needs first.
4. Scams promise too much or ask for passwords: say no and tell a grown-up.
