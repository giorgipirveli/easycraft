## What a child sees and does

**The Life tab.** SCHOOL on the main menu, then **LIFE**, the 13th tab on every grade's map (its own deep green and a
heart). The first time: Professor Hoot asks "Which one are you?" with three big pictures, **LITTLE** (about 6 to 8),
**MIDDLE** (9 to 11) and **BIG** (12 and up), read aloud to every child. Then the **map**: the ten areas as picture cards
in Life's two strands, **LIVING WELL** (my body and health, my feelings, me and other people, staying safe, life online,
home and everyday, getting around, our world) and **SUCCESS** (money and business; thinking and the future). An area
shows its units on the child's track (EASIER and HARDER look at the others without changing the child's own). A unit has
**LESSON** and **PRACTICE** in the School's format (its stars, gold for answers right the first time and 100 once when
mastered), and an Experience's unit has **TRY IT**, its Passport stamps, and **EXPERT TRY** once done by the child alone.
The other areas' units are the School's own lessons where they fit (60 of them, one unit one reward), or "Coming soon"
for L2's Experiences (Safe Streets, The Kitchen, Calm Corner). **PASSPORT** (the Life Passport: a page per skill, three
stamps each) and **GROWN-UPS** (behind the parent gate) sit on the map's header.

**TRY IT.** The first time: "Before we start: what do you know already? Any answer is fine!": 3-5 picture questions
(nothing is marked). Then the **practice room** opens (the School hides; in VR the child stands in the room). At the
top: Professor Hoot with the step's words (read aloud on Little; a speaker on every track), "3 of 12" and a little bar;
**SHOW ME** and **I NEED A BREAK** on the right, always. The one next thing glows (a soft warm shell). A miss shows the
step again with a kind line; after 10 s without progress the coach shows it (a ghost finger on a screen, ghost hands in
VR). At the end: the **name it** card (the steps in the authorities' own words, the source under them, read aloud),
the same picture questions again, then the result: the stamps (an **EXPERT!** surprise with confetti on a new Expert
stamp), "+100 gold for Survivor!" the first time, the honest line ("knew 1 of 5 at the start, now 5 of 5, in 9
minutes") and the **Try it for real** card ("... Show your grown-up how!"). I NEED A BREAK: "Take a break. That's fine!
You can carry on now, or stop for now. Nothing is lost."; STOP FOR NOW: "Well done for practising. You can come back
any time: nothing is lost."

**Flat and VR, the same steps.** Flat: a finger (or the mouse) on the thing does it, near is near enough (a fingertip's
room at least); holding is holding the finger down; a sweep is a drag; GET LOW (bottom left) drops the view under the
smoke; an action's big picture button (STOP, COVER FACE, ROLL); taps to the beat. VR: the child stands at each step's
spot (a blink when it moves); "the hand is here and squeezing" (grip or trigger) presses or holds the nearest thing it
touches; the laser and the trigger press farther things, aim, and sweep; getting really low (the head 30 cm down) is
low; both hands on the chest pushing down and up are pushes, and both controllers buzz (strong on a push at the beat,
soft off it); both hands at the face cover it; the head side to side while low rolls; the HUD is on the tray, its
buttons pressed with the laser. A child who can't crouch gets GET LOW and the action buttons on the panel after the
coach has shown the step twice.

### Little Lifesaver (the first aid room)

A bright training room: soft blue mats, a low training table, a side table with the play phone and the first aid kit,
a sink, a green-cross poster, "PRACTICE ROOM" on the wall.
- **Little, Call for help:** Bobo the buddy has slipped and sits on the floor ("This is practice. A real first aid course
  teaches more."). Is it safe? (pictures) → touch Bobo's shoulder: "are you all right?" (Bobo: "ouch, my leg!") → pick up
  the phone (it comes up in front of the child, big) → press the region's number on its big keys (112 in Georgia: "one
  one two") → the calm helper's questions as pictures: what happened? where are you? ("My address (I learn it with my
  grown-up)": never typed; "Never type it into a game") → is your friend awake? → stay on the line → hold Bobo's hand 3 s
  → a helper buddy walks in and nods. Expert try: the helper also asks which helpers you need (ambulance).
- **Middle, Cuts, burns and the recovery position:** Bobo on a stool with soft glowing "ouch" spots (never a wound):
  gloves → a clean cloth → press on the cut 3 s → a plaster → the sink: the cool water on, the paw under it, a clock
  speeding through **20 minutes** → cling film, loosely, and tell a grown-up → "let's pretend Bobo is breathing but won't
  wake up": the near arm out like an L, the other hand at the cheek, the far knee up, pull the knee and Bobo rolls onto
  the side, tilt the head back → call the number and stay. Expert try: a second cut, and "which one is right for a
  burn?" (cool running water; never ice or butter).
- **Big, Hands-only CPR and choking:** Max the training dummy on the table: is it safe? → touch both shoulders, "are
  you all right?" → look, listen and feel for normal breathing (VR: the head close to Max's face) → not breathing
  normally: call, then push → the phone on speaker → both hands on the middle of the chest → **30 pushes, at least 20 at
  100-120 a minute** (the beat meter: a pulsing ring, a gauge with the green band, "12 of 30 pushes"; the chest goes down
  and up; VR buzzes) → swap helpers about every 2 minutes → Lulu the buddy is coughing: keep coughing → she can't: 5 back
  blows, then 5 tummy thrusts → it's out: a doctor should check her. Expert try: 25 good pushes, and the defibrillator
  arrives ("switch it on and do what its voice says").

### Fire Escape (the practice house)

A bedroom, a living room with its kitchen corner, and the garden where both ways out lead to the meeting point (a big
tree with "MEETING POINT"); the smoke alarm beeps softly; the smoke is a soft grey haze under the ceiling; no fire is
ever shown inside.
- **Little, Spot the dangers, get out:** the living room as a cut-away doll's house (no ceiling or front wall: all of it
  in one view): find 10 of 12 things that could start a fire (a pan handle sticking out, a candle by the curtains, a towel
  on the heater, too many plugs, an iron left on, matches, a lighter on the sofa, paper by the toaster, a cloth over a
  lamp, the smoke alarm needing a test, a tea towel by the hob, a phone charging under a pillow): each gets a green tick
  and its own line → the drill: the alarm beeps → get out (not hide, not toys) → get low → out the front door → stand up
  at the meeting point → who calls the number (from outside) → Teddy is inside: stay out, tell a firefighter. Expert try:
  all 12, and boxes block the front door: out through the garden door.
- **Middle, Fire escape, and stop, drop and roll:** night, in bed, the alarm: get out → get low → touch the door with the
  back of your hand (cool) → open it, through, staying low → close it behind you → the front door → stand up at the
  meeting point → the garden phone: press the number → Teddy: stay out → stop (stand still), drop, cover your face, roll.
  Expert try: the bedroom door is warm: leave it shut, the garden door instead (check it, open it, close it behind you).
- **Big, Fire escape and the extinguisher:** the quick escape (the warm door on the Expert try) → at the meeting point:
  call from out here (never back in for a phone) → when may a grown-up use an extinguisher (a small fire, the way out
  behind) → a small cartoon flame in a metal bin outside: **P**ull the pin, **A**im at the bottom (1 s), **S**queeze the
  handle, **S**weep side to side (4 sweeps; 6 on the Expert try) → back away, tell a grown-up → a growing fire: get out,
  stay out, call.

### My Money Stall (the market; My Business's first step)

A sunny market square: the child's stand under a striped awning (the goods' crate, the cash box with its count, the
coin tray of **pretend market coins** 1, 2, 5, 10 and a 20 note, the saving jar marked ROCKET), buddy customers across
the counter, the **PRETEND BANK**, the SHOP with price tags and PAY HERE.
- **Little, coins and saving:** put the goods out → a fair price (2 coins, not 20 or 200) → Pip-pop pays 2: count her
  coins into the cash box → give her the bread, say thank you → Mo pays 4 or 5: give the change coin by coin (too much
  comes back) → how many coins in the cash box now? → put 2 in the saving jar for the rocket → need or want (water or a
  toy robot). Expert try: a stranger's "give me all your coins, tomorrow I'll give you a thousand!": no, thank you.
- **Middle, change, the bank, smart shopping:** a price with a profit (3: the cost of 2 and a little more) → a sale for
  3, 6 or 9 paid with a 10: the change → the day's takings → to the bank: deposit 10 or 20 → "this pretend bank pays 1 coin
  a week for every 10 you keep (real banks pay much less)": 3 weeks go by → how much now (13 or 26) → the shop: bread,
  milk and apples within 10 coins, pay at the till → "give me 10 coins now, 100 tomorrow": a trick. Expert try: only 8
  coins for the shopping, and a "send us your password" message.
- **Big, profit, interest and scams:** profit = sales - costs (Expert: the costs went up) → change from a 20 note →
  deposit 100 → compound interest, 10% a week for 3 weeks: 133 (not 130: interest on the interest) → a week's lunches
  within 20 (Expert: 12) → "double your coins in one day, guaranteed!" and a password message: say no, tell a grown-up.
  "For real money, talk to a grown-up you trust."

The money varies with each try's seed (the prices, the customers, the deposit), so a repeat comes back a little
different; the choices are always shown in a fresh order (the data writes the right one first).

## Real motions and devices, as data (owner #117, the design's §13)

The owner (#117): "they should be able to use any instrument and devices ... we must use same aproach for any skills".
The design (§13 on lead-merge) gives every skill one shared engine later: ten real tool motions recognised from the hand
(strike, stroke, twist, squeeze, press, spread, measure, pour, aim, cut) and one device model (controls, what it shows,
the right order, the safety steps). L1 builds **no engine**: each hands-on step only **names its motion** as data
(`motion`, one of `LifeCheck.MOTIONS`; the rules never read it), the thing in the hand (`tool`: cloth, plaster, cling
film, gloves, the tap, coins) and its device (`device`), so the shared recogniser can take over without a rule changing.
CPR, the cloth on a cut, a plaster, the shoulders, the door's back-of-hand check and closing it, tummy thrusts, the
keys, every coin and the shopping: **press**; a back blow: **strike**; the tap, a door's handle and the extinguisher's
pin: **twist**; holding Bobo's hand and the extinguisher's handle: **squeeze**; cling film: **spread**; aiming and
sweeping the extinguisher: **aim**. The devices (`devices` in each Experience's JSON, `LifeExperience.devices`): the
**play phone** (its keys and speaker, its screen, the order pick up → the number → what and where → the questions →
stay on; only for a real emergency, an address said and never typed; Fire Escape's: call from outside), the
**extinguisher** (pin, handle, hose; pull, aim at the bottom, squeeze, sweep; a small fire only, the way out behind, a
grown-up uses it, if in doubt get out) and the **shop's till** (PAY, the total; needs first, add up, pay; within the
budget). Tests: every hands-on kind of step names one of the ten; the motions the owner named; every device has its
four parts and every step's device exists. The review sheet shows them all.

## Rulings (decided, with what each costs if wrong)

1. **Life is its own screen inside the School, not Curriculum units.** Its progress is in `ProfileData.life`; nothing is
   sent to the friends' server (no SocialSender for Life), as the brief asks; a School unit shown in Life keeps the
   School's own progress and reward. *Cost:* a later Life wish for postcards or friends' cheers needs a hook.
2. **The track** is the child's choice, saved; EASIER and HARDER only look; a grown-up's lock disables the picker. *Cost:*
   one flag.
3. **The region list** has 10 entries; an unknown country is Europe's 112; Ireland, Malta and Cyprus are one entry (112,
   left-hand traffic). Japan's entry is 119 (fire and ambulance: what the Experiences call). *Cost:* a country with another
   number sees 112 until a grown-up sets it; the list grows by data.
4. **The careful switches** merge as the newer copy's (the grown-up's latest choice); the lock merges as "either". *Cost:*
   an older device's switch can be overwritten by a newer backup.
5. **Gold:** an Experience's first finished run of a skill pays 100 (`EarnRules.UNIT_GOLD`, "like a lesson"); stamps pay
   nothing themselves (research §1.8); Life's units pay like the School's (5 an answer inside the shared daily cap, 100
   once on mastery). No `EarnRules.TABLE` row yet (core/economy is not mine): the Gold Shop's list doesn't name Life.
   *Cost:* constants (`LifePassport.GOLD`); one row for the lead.
6. **Stamps:** Done it = finished; By myself = finished with no hint at all (an automatic coach, SHOW ME and a miss each
   count); Expert = the Expert try with no hint (it also stamps By myself); the Expert try opens after By myself. *Cost:*
   a child who peeks once gets Done it for that run (and can try again at once).
7. **The coach** shows after 10 s without progress (20 s on the Expert try), again every 10 s; words and time-lapses
   never count hints. *Cost:* constants (`LifeRun`).
8. **The checks:** the before-check only the first time per skill (never marked); the after-check after every finished
   run (its best and latest kept); leaving the after-check still shows the result. *Cost:* one condition.
9. **Spaced repeats** 1, 3, 7, 21 and 60 days after the first finished run ("practise again" on the map, the area and the
   Passport); the variation is the money's seed, the choices' order and any 10 of the 12 hazards. *Cost:* constants;
   richer variation (another room, another twist) is data later.
10. **Pretend market coins**, never the child's gold; the pretend bank pays 10% a week, simple on Middle, compound on Big,
    and says real banks pay much less. *Cost:* numbers in `LifeMoney`.
11. **The goods are given** ("your farm's wheat made bread"), not taken from the child's world bag (the School can't reach
    a world's inventory). *Cost:* a later link to the farm.
12. **VR squeezing** is the grip or the trigger (on at 0.6, off under 0.35) and presses only the nearest thing the hand
    touches; the laser presses what is farther (not on "hand" steps) up to 6 m; low is the head 30 cm under the spot's
    eye; GET LOW and the action buttons appear in VR after two coach showings (seated children). *Cost:* constants in
    `LifeRoomHost`, `LifeReach`.
13. **A VR push** is the hands down 3.5 cm and up 2 cm (a controller can't measure a chest's 5-6 cm, which the lesson
    says); 30 pushes with 20 at the beat (25 on the Expert try). *Cost:* constants (`LifeBeat`, the data).
14. **The rooms live in the main world, 1.5 km above the menu** (`ROOM_ORIGIN`), with their own environment (the camera's
    and, in VR, `Vr.view_like`); the menu's CanvasLayer is hidden while a room shows. The menu's sun still lights them
    (the rooms cast no shadows of their own). *Cost:* if a world ever reaches 1500 m there, move the origin.
15. **Never an injury, never a scary fire:** "ouch" spots, a grey haze, a cartoon flame outside; framed as practice in the
    first words, on the card and on the grown-ups' page. *Cost:* none.
16. **The extinguisher on the Big track** as the brief asks, framed "a grown-up uses it on a small fire with the way out
    behind; if in doubt, get out" (the fire services tell children to get out). *Cost:* if the fire safety officer
    prefers, PASS becomes knowledge only (one data change).
17. **Choking's tummy thrusts** are on the Big track (12+), on Lulu, with "a doctor should check them". *Cost:* wording if
    the first aid trainer wants "a grown-up does this".
18. **The units:** 9 of Life's own (one per Experience track, the School's quality bar: a memory question first, "Try it"
    last, 11-12 exercises on three levels), 60 of the School's own where they fit, "coming soon" only for L2's three
    Experiences. Getting around's Little track has only Safe Streets' stub (no School unit fits). *Cost:* data.
19. **The hazard hunt is a cut-away doll's house** (the Little track's living room has no ceiling or front wall), so all
    12 things show from one view. *Cost:* the Little VR house is roofless (sky above: calm).
20. **The measurements** stay in `user://life_measure.json` only (not the profile, not the backup): per step its runs,
    those without a hint, the seconds, and how the very first time went. *Cost:* a playtest export button later.
21. **Read aloud:** the Little track speaks every line (cutting in: the newest matters), the others by Read to me or a
    speaker; the track question is read to every child. *Cost:* one condition.
22. **Only warmed shaders** in the rooms (the avatar's lit material, the mirror wall's see-through settings, the animals'
    billboard tags, a plain colour background). *Cost:* the props are a little shinier; signs always face the camera.
23. **Android Back in a room** is the menu's own Back (it leaves the School: `menu.gd` is not mine); I NEED A BREAK is the
    calm way out. *Cost:* for the lead: `Menu._notification` could ask the Life room first.
24. **A game controller** can press the HUD's buttons but not a room's 3D things yet (touch, mouse and VR can). *Cost:*
    pad players on a PC or TV can't do the touch steps: a task for the ease audit (E0).
25. **No blocks** were needed (page 45 unused).
26. **Motions are named where one fits** (owner #117): moving a buddy's arm or knee, getting low, rolling, finding a hazard
    and going somewhere name none (they are the body, the laser or a walk, not a tool's motion); giving change and every
    tap is "press" for now. *Cost:* one data field per step when the shared recogniser wants another word.
27. **In a headset LIFE sits in the School map's header** (the full suite's VR readability audit: as a 13th tab the map
    needed 1366 px of the 1280 px VR canvas at 24 px words); on screens it stays the 13th tab. *Cost:* the two places
    differ; one hook.
