2. Afterwards: walk out to open ground, away from buildings, trees and power lines.
3. Flood water: turn around, never walk or ride through it.
4. Thunder: get inside a building or a car, never under a lone tree.
5. Heat: shade, water and rest.

Its source line: ShakeOut, FEMA's Ready.gov, the US National Weather Service, the UK Environment Agency and the WHO (heat and health)

Try it for real: With a grown-up: find the safe spots at home (under a strong table, by an inside wall) and the open place outside where your family would meet.

Your child can now: drop, cover and hold on, keep out of flood water, and stay safe in thunder and heat

The "name it" card: **Ready for anything**

1. Be ready: an emergency bag and a family plan.
2. Drop, cover, hold on; shoes on before you walk; then out to open ground.
3. Thunder: go down, away from peaks, lone trees and water, and get inside.
4. Lost: stay put, be seen (bright colours) and heard (a whistle).
5. Cold: layers, a hat, keep dry. Heat: shade, water, rest.

Its source line: ShakeOut, FEMA's Ready.gov, the US National Weather Service, mountain rescue advice and Hug-a-Tree and Survive

Try it for real: With a grown-up: pack (or check) your family's emergency bag together, and agree where you would all meet.
