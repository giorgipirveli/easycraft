For the review by an electrical safety specialist (Electrical Safety First or the national electrical safety body) and the electricity network's safety officer (fallen lines, substations), with a first aid trainer for electric shock.

The device **phone** (owner #117, the device model): controls: the keys 0 to 9; the speaker. It shows: the numbers pressed; CALLING.... The device **fuse_box** (owner #117, the device model): controls: the main switch; the RCD and its test button; the circuit switches. It shows: each switch up (on) or down (off); the labels of the circuits. The right order: a grown-up opens the cover → the main switch off turns all the power off → the RCD's test button: it switches itself off, then back on → close the cover. The device **rcd** (owner #117, the device model): controls: the test button, marked T; the switch. It shows: the switch up (on) or down (off). Safety: a grown-up tests it, as its label says (often every three months); if it doesn't switch off, call an electrician.
