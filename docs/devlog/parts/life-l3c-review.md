The device **gates** (owner #117, the device model): controls: the reader where you tap your ticket. It shows: a green light and an arrow: go through; a red cross: ask the staff for help. The right order: tap your ticket on the reader → wait for the green light → walk through, one person at a time. Safety: one person at a time; on the platform, stand behind the yellow line.
The device **help_point** (owner #117, the device model): controls: the help button. It shows: a light while the staff are talking; the speaker. The right order: press the help button → say you're separated from your group and which station you're at → do what the staff say. Safety: stay where the staff can find you; only for help.

The "name it" card: **Stay, ask, follow**

1. Lost? Stop, and stay where you are: your grown-up will come back to look.
2. Ask a safe grown-up: a worker with a name badge, a parent with children, a police officer.
3. Never go off with anyone, even if they say they'll help.
4. A fire drill: leave your things and follow your teacher calmly to the meeting point.

Its source line: the police's and child-safety charities' advice (NCMEC, Kidpower) and the fire and rescue services

Try it for real: Say your address to your grown-up now, and your grown-up's phone number: they'll tell you if it's right. Never type them into a game or a website. Then agree where you'd meet if you got separated in a shop.

Your child can now: stay where they are if they get lost, find a safe grown-up to ask, follow the teacher in a fire drill and remember the way by landmarks

(NCMEC (the US missing children's organisation), child safety): https://www.missingkids.org/
- [ ] Children who are lost can ask a mother or father with children for help, stay in the same place, and should never go off with anyone, even someone offering to help. (Kidpower, getting lost): https://www.kidpower.org/library/article/getting-lost/
