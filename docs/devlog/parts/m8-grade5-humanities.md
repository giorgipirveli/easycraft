## Generators (`core/school/gen/g5_humanities_gen.gd`, `G5HumanitiesGen`, SchoolGen source "g5h", the last SOURCES line)
Seeded, levels 1-3, each with a hint, a worked answer and `data`:
- `olympiad`: the 2nd-5th Games' year (1), later Games (2), which Games were held in a year, or how many Games from
  776 BC to a year, counting both (3).
- `classical_years`: two BC events (1), two AD events or two BC events (2), a BC event to an AD event, exactly, "there
  is no year 0" (3); `classical_order`: three events 150+ years apart (1), four BC events (2), four across BC and AD
  (3). `EVENTS` holds the lessons' 26 dated events (with "about" where the lessons say it).
- `roman_numeral`: read to 39 on a milestone (1); 40-399, read or written (2); years 1000-2100, read or written, the
  wrong choices a numeral turned round (XL for LX) and near numbers (3).
- `maya_number`: one place (1), two places labelled, sometimes a shell (2), three places unlabelled (3), on the
  numerals picture.
- `lat_long`: places A-D on a 30° grid: which place is at a latitude and longitude, or south of the Equator (1); a
  place's latitude or longitude on a 15° grid (2); a real city's hemispheres, or the city nearest the Equator (3).
- `time_zone`: January clocks from London to a city 1-4 hours ahead, the same day (1); to any city, ahead or behind,
  across midnight (2); between two cities given against London, or a longitude's Sun time at 15° an hour (3); a digital
  clock shows the start time.
- `altitude_temp`: 6.5 °C per 1,000 m: 2,000 or 4,000 m up, whole (1); 1,000-4,000 m, halves (2); below freezing, or
  how high 0 °C is (3).
- `resource_sort`: renewable or non-renewable, 4 easy (1), 5 harder (2); fossil fuel, metal ore or from living things
  (3). `atlas_index`: an alphabetical index in a table: a page (1); a square, or the place in a square (2); latitudes
  and longitudes: furthest north, south of the Equator, west of Greenwich (3). `trade_count`: exports and imports (1),
  containers with a part-full one (2), a voyage's days (3).
- `election` (a table of votes: the winner; the margin or the total; the turnout as a percentage, or a majority, both
  ways), `budget_plan` (what is left; needs, saving, wants; a budget table: the saving, or weeks to a goal),
  `simple_interest` (10% or 5% for a year; the account after a year at 2-5%; 2-4 years), `loan_cost` (the cost of a
  loan; paying back after a year at 5-20%; equal repayments, or which of two loans costs less), `waste_change` (a
  table of four weeks' rubbish: how much less; the total; the percentage fall, always whole).

## Rulings (decide and write down; cost if wrong)
1. **Every lesson opens with "Quick memory:" and ends with "Try it in the world: … Next: …"** as in grade 4, and the
   test enforces it. *Cost:* none.
2. **Dates are given as the lessons give them**: "about" where historians give a range (550, 508, 321, 268 and 138 BC;
   AD 60, 250, 350, 410 and 900), legends and traditions called so (Rome's founding in 753 BC "says the legend"; 776 BC
   is "the first Olympic Games we know of"), exact ones exact. `EVENTS` holds the same dates. *Cost:* none.
3. **The no-year-0 rule** (taught in grade 4) is practised at level 3 (the Olympic Games to 1896, Caesar to Claudius,
   Augustus to 476, the Han dynasty's 425 years). *Cost:* none.
4. **The world grid has no coastlines**: the repo has no world outline and none was added (only CC0 assets); places
   are dots with their names, which is what latitude and longitude teach. *Cost:* a land layer (a CC0 outline) later.
5. **Maya numbers and the world grid live in `SchoolPictureHistory`** (a "history" class now also drawing a geography
   kind) so that `school_picture.gd` needed no new dispatch lines and merges cleanly. *Cost:* a rename to
   `SchoolPictureHumanities` later.
6. **Time zones are January's** (standard time) against London, and levels 1-2 always give the hours in the prompt:
   children learn the method, not a table to memorise. Cities whose clocks change in January (Sydney, Auckland,
   Santiago) are left out of the generator; India's 5½ hours and summer time are taught in words. *Cost:* a table
   line per city.
7. **Maya counting places are 1, 20, 400** (the counting system); the calendar's 360 place is not taught. *Cost:* a
   card in grade 7 or 8 if wanted.
8. **Real people are historical only** (Cleisthenes to Brahmagupta; Rosa Parks, 1955); no living people. Religions are
   beliefs people hold (Zoroastrianism, the Buddha's teachings and dhamma, the six largest religions and "no
   religion"); festivals come together from many traditions (research §5.4 rule 1). *Cost:* none.
9. **Hard history is named, never shown**: many people in Athens were enslaved; the Kalinga war was "terrible" in
   Ashoka's own words; Boudica's family was "treated cruelly"; Caesar was "killed by senators"; no violence described.
   *Cost:* none.
10. **Grade 5's drugs, alcohol and gambling rule (research §5.4 rule 5)** is used twice, factually: a law protecting
    children's health (shops may not sell alcohol or tobacco to children) and a budget fact (betting games are made so
    that most players lose more than they win). Nothing shows use. *Cost:* two sentences if the owner wants none.
11. **Places and borders stay neutral** (§5.4 rule 2): today's countries are named only as places (Iran, Iraq,
    Turkey); only undisputed capitals are called capitals (Quito); Bolivia's La Paz and the gulf's disputed name are
    avoided; "nobody lives in Antarctica for good" (families at a few stations come and go). *Cost:* none.
12. **Money is the game's gold** (research §5.5 ruling 6), and the content test now also bans real currencies' names
    and "$" in these units. Interest is simple in grade 5 (compound is grade 6's `compound_interest`), and every
    percentage in practice is whole (grade 5 maths' `percent_intro`). *Cost:* none.
13. **Up a mountain it is about 6.5 °C colder for every 1,000 m**, grade 4's figure, in lessons and `altitude_temp`.
    *Cost:* none.
14. **Online safety in four units** (location privacy, bank passwords and messages, never borrowing from strangers,
    teasing online with the game's REPORT and BLOCK). *Cost:* none.
15. **Rounded facts say "about"**: the Royal Road's 2,700 km, 111 stations, 90 and 7 days (Herodotus); Hadrian's
    Wall's 117 km; the Alps' 1,200 km and the Andes' 7,000 km; Mont Blanc about 4,800 m (as in grade 4); rain in
    Singapore (over 2,000 mm), Cairo (about 20 mm) and London (about 600 mm). *Cost:* none.
16. **The number pad groups every number** ("1,850"), so a year typed for MDCCCL shows with a comma. This is the
    School's shared pad, not changed here. *Cost:* a "year" flag on number exercises, if the owner minds.
17. **The unit files are the source of truth**; the Python that wrote them (with its own limits, letters, kind words,
    US spellings, currencies and sums checks) stayed in the agent's scratchpad.
