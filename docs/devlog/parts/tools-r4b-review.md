# Real Builder: the review sheet for the experts (owner #116, #117)

Written by `tools/real_builder_review_sheet.py` from Real Builder's own data (`data/tools/real_builder.json`) and the Tool Book (`data/tools/tools.json`): exactly what the game shows and reads aloud. For the review by a bricklaying trainer (the Brick Development Association, a construction college) and a site safety adviser (the HSE's construction guidance). Please mark each step: right / change it to ... / remove it. Every tool step names the real motion it is done with (strike, stroke, twist, squeeze, press, spread, measure, pour, aim, cut) and the numbers the game checks (metres, m/s, radians, seconds).

The device **mixer**: controls: the on and off switch; the bucket of water; the shovel of sand; the bag of cement. It shows: the drum; the mix. The right order: gloves on → goggles on → switch it on → a little water first → four shovels of sand → one shovel of cement → more water, until it is like soft butter → switch it off before it is tipped out. Safety: gloves on: wet cement burns skin; goggles on: cement dust hurts eyes; never hands or a shovel inside while it turns.

## The Tool Book's words for these tools

- **hard hat** (safety gear): for protecting your head on a building site. How: Straight on your head, the strap done up. Safety: On a building site it stays on, all the time.
- **gloves** (safety gear): for keeping hands safe and clean: work gloves for building, thin gloves for first aid. How: Pull them all the way on, one finger in each. Safety: Thin first aid gloves are used once, then thrown away.
- **trowel** (spread, strike): for spreading mortar, and tapping bricks down to the line. How: Hold the handle in your fist, thumb on top, the blade low and flat. Safety: Gloves on: wet mortar is rough on skin.
- **spirit level** (aim): for checking that a row is level and a wall is straight up. How: Lay it along the bricks and keep it still, then read the bubble. Safety: Carry it by the middle, so its ends never swing into anyone.
- **safety goggles** (safety gear): for keeping dust and chips out of your eyes. How: Over your eyes, the strap snug round your head. Safety: Goggles on before you saw, drill or hammer.
- **tape measure** (measure): for measuring how long, wide or high something is. How: Hook the end on the start, then pull the tape straight to the end. Safety: Let the tape roll back slowly: its edge can nip fingers.
- **plumb line** (aim): for checking that a wall is straight up and down. How: Hold the string at the top of the wall and let the weight hang still. Safety: Let the weight hang: never swing it.
- **handsaw** (stroke): for cutting wood to length. How: One hand on the handle, first finger pointing along it: long, slow strokes. Safety: Goggles on, the wood held tight, your other hand well away from the teeth.
- **claw hammer** (strike): for driving nails in, and pulling them out with the claw. How: Hold it near the end of the handle and start with little taps. Safety: Goggles on; let go of the nail once it stands up by itself.
