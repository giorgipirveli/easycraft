# EasyCraft 0.3.0 — what's new (for the owner's phone)

File: `~/Downloads/EasyCraft-0.3.0.apk` (arm64 phones; installs over 0.2.0 — the version code went up,
your Sandbox world stays). This is **M2 "Survive the Night"** plus the parts of M3 and M4 that were
finished and tested on the emulator when it was built.

## Try these
1. **SURVIVOR** on the main menu → pick COZY, NORMAL or BRAVE. You start with a small kit, hearts and 25 gold.
2. Break blocks to collect them; **Tilly the trader** (her stall is near the start) sells tools, doors,
   lanterns and potions for gold.
3. At night **monsters** come (slimes and goblins). Crouch to be quieter, hide in a **bush, barrel or
   closet**; fight with the sword, bow, spear or mace. An arrow at the screen edge shows one coming from behind.
4. If you're knocked out you wake up at home (your bed) and your blocks wait in a bag where you fell.
5. The **Monster Totem**: start waves for gold; after wave 5 the **Stone Golem** comes. Beat him and a
   **portal** opens to the **Sandstorm Desert** (universe 2: pyramids, mummies, scorpions, sandstorms,
   the Sand Serpent).
6. **Bot friends**: pause → FRIENDS → add a builder, explorer or guard who plays with you.
7. **Secrets**: buried treasure (your pet dog sniffs it out), hidden temples, crystal geodes, golden
   slimes; the BOOK button counts what's still hidden.
8. Animals, monsters and bot friends now have **little minds**: they learn — an animal you feed comes to
   you, goblins you shoot from far learn to zigzag, a builder friend learns your favourite blocks.
9. The **new buttons**: every button has a picture and a word; rarely used ones are in **MORE**.
10. Sandbox too: gentle monsters at night, the Physics **LAB** (moon gravity, zero-G…) in MORE, photo
    mode, emotes, the avatar editor (pause → AVATAR).

## Not in this build yet
- **Online play** needs your internet server first (`docs/hosting.md` explains it step by step);
  until then PLAY ONLINE says it isn't available. LAN play at home works (same Wi-Fi).
- Frostpeak and the Candy Kingdom (universes 3–4), smart blocks and races, levels and the adventure
  board, pets with perks, the tutorial — being built now (M3).
