# EasyCraft 0.3.2 — what's new (2026-09-24, the first Google Play build)
File: `~/Downloads/EasyCraft-0.3.2-play.aab` (Android App Bundle for Google Play's internal testing, package
`com.giorgi.easycraft`, versionCode 7, signed with the upload key). The developer APKs keep `com.giorgi.game`.
- **New modes:** MONSTER WAVE (#44): a flat arena where you survive waves for gold, with a raid boss at wave 10 and
  then the next universe if you like. Survivor has no waves any more. Survivor games: CLASSIC or BUILD TO SURVIVE
  (#33). Crafting (#34): a crafting table, a furnace and a picture recipe book, on the CRAFT tab.
- **New universe:** the Candy Kingdom (frosting plains, chocolate rivers, gumdrop hills, gummy bears, candy sneakers,
  the Cake Monster).
- **Sounds and music (#27, #31):** classical music (Beethoven, Mozart, Bach, Grieg, Tchaikovsky and more, with a
  MUSIC setting: game, classical or mix), real animal voices, monster voices, footsteps, ambience, button clicks.
- **Treasures (#42):** shipwrecks, sunken chests, mineshafts, vaults with a lever puzzle, a friendly haunted house,
  wishing wells, rainbows with a pot of gold, and chest rarities.
- **Toy blasters** (Sandbox, free; Survivor, from Tilly), the Party Piñata, supply balloons and goblin archers.
- **Chat:** 21 new commands (#40), and bot friends understand what you say (#38: "follow me", "wait here",
  "build a house"…).
- **With friends:** hide-and-seek, Storm Run, the team photo after a boss; gifts, showcase visits and bottle mail.
- **Online:** release builds use the new internet server.
