# EasyCraft 0.3.3 — what's new (2026-09-25, a hotfix for the testers)
0.3.2 plus the two fixes from the owner's first online play on phones (23:37). Nothing else changed.
- **Chat you can read (#49):** while the chat is open it sits on top of everything, so the words you type are
  never hidden. The newest lines stay above the keyboard, every line has a dark backing, Georgian letters use
  the phone's own font, and for younger players the lines move beside the picture-phrase wheel.
- **Calmer animals (#50):** at most one animal call every 6–10 s near you, the animals take turns, hens are
  softer, the nearest animals call first and far ones are quieter, and no sound plays the same take twice in a
  row. Petting and bumping animals still sound at once.
Files: `~/Downloads/EasyCraft-0.3.3.apk` (developer package) and `~/Downloads/EasyCraft-0.3.3-play.aab` (Google
Play, versionCode 8).
