# EasyCraft 0.4.0 — what's new (2026-09-25; M3 "Worlds of Wonder" and M4 "Better Together" nearly complete)
Since 0.3.3. The M3 acceptance run, the tutorial with Pip and M5's polish (accessibility, performance and size, store
art) come next; 1.0.0 is the release candidate after them.

**Worlds and places**
- **Universe portals you build:** put a universe's crystal in a portal frame (golden, with rainbow gems) and walk
  through; a Sandbox world for every universe (#39).
- **The Dragon Realm** (universe 5): floating islands at sunrise over a sea of clouds, shadow bats and wisps, the Sky
  Dragon with its sun lanterns, dragon nests, and a baby pet dragon to win (#39, #43).
- **A bigger world** for new worlds: birch and dark forests, flower fields, swamps, beaches, a sea with islands,
  rivers that reach the sea, snowy mountains, glowing mushroom caves; villages with villagers, windmills, lighthouses,
  towers, ships, castle ruins and towns; MY MAP of the places you found (#45).
- **The Tiny world:** hidden mouse villages only a tiny child can walk into; pumpkins, cacti and snowmen that wake up;
  a chest that hops after you (21c).

**Things to do**
- **Quests:** Tilly, Zara, Bram, Mimi and Nell the dragon keeper tell 11 picture stories with keepsakes, titles and
  places; 44 new adventure-board goals; the quest book in MORE (#45).
- **19 new animals** with real voices: horses and camels to ride, a donkey with a chest, goats, deer, ducks, frogs,
  turtles with eggs, squirrels, owls, hedgehogs, lizards, fennecs, penguins, a gentle polar bear, seals, gummy bunnies
  and candy birds (#41).
- **Buddy magic:** 8 secret buddy shapes, the Buddy Album, My Stamps, befriending calm monsters, mini-buddies after a
  boss, and tricks your buddies learn from your dances.
- **Building tools:** paint (16 colours), LINE, FILL, COPY and PASTE, Build Rewind (a time-lapse of your build), and
  wearing what you build as a hat, backpack or wings.
- **Trading:** find Tilly from MORE → SHOP, a stand by every helper, market day, and trading with friends (#37).
- **The books:** the Secret Book's new Animal Book, Memory Book, idea cards and Saving up; trophy shelves and a
  bookshelf; the Hub Tree that grows with you.
- **Ghost races:** race a friend's best run on their showcase, in a present, even offline.

**You**
- **Girl avatars:** BOY / GIRL / JUST ME, 19 new hair styles, clothes and faces, ready-made looks (#48).
- **21 storybook costumes** from books and our own designs, earned by playing (#36).
- **Our own look:** a new icon, the EasyCraft title, our own loading screen, a new default avatar with a buddy badge,
  a Workbench and a Clay Oven (#43).
- **ქართული / English:** the whole game in Georgian (pick it at the start or in SETTINGS), read-aloud buttons,
  babble voices for friends and buddies, and Simple controls for younger children.

**Play notes (for the Play Console's "What's new", under 500 characters):**
New worlds to explore: build portals to five universes, including the Dragon Realm! Villages, rivers to the sea,
19 new animals to ride and befriend, quests with Tilly and friends, storybook costumes, girl avatars, trading, ghost
races, building tools, and the whole game in Georgian.
