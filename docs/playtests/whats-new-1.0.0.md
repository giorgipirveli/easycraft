# EasyCraft 1.0.0 — what's new (the release candidate, 2026-09-26)
Since 0.4.0. M3 "Worlds of Wonder", M4 "Better Together" and M5 "Launch" are complete (everything but the iOS build).

**New for players**
- **Pip teaches you:** the first time you play Survivor, Pip (your robot friend) offers a short lesson in a playground
  near the start: walk, jump a gap, build a bridge, break a wall, hide in a bush, make sticks, find Tilly. The reward is
  500 gold, Pip's Cap and Pip as your first pet. Play it again any time: SETTINGS → HELP → PIP'S LESSONS.
- **Summon creatures in the Sandbox:** the "+" palette has a MOBS tab with every animal, monster and boss as a picture;
  a tap brings it out in front of you (#51).
- **Fair weapons:** a weapon's strength now follows how hard it is to get. The rarer its colour, the harder it hits; the
  blasters (the legendary Confetti Rocket most of all) got much stronger (#53).
- **Play your way (accessibility):** SETTINGS → BUTTONS & TEXT: bigger buttons (BIG, BIGGER), a left-hand layout,
  MOVE BUTTONS to drag JUMP, ATTACK, CROUCH and the stick where you like, bigger text, and sound captions (little
  pictures when a monster, a boss or the night is coming). Colours never carry meaning alone any more.
- **A livelier game:** a floating island with a sleepy buddy under the title that wakes up and waves; every universe
  has its own picture on its card and behind its menus; loading screens show a hint with a picture; slimes crouch
  before they pounce, monsters stagger when hit, the giant bosses breathe and cheer; Frostpeak and the Candy Kingdom
  have their own sounds and music, and night monsters grumble less often.
- **Smaller and faster:** the game is about half the size on your phone (about 97 MB instead of 194 MB), weak phones
  start on LOW (shorter view, no shadows, fewer monsters at once), and busy moments are smoother.

**Safer together**
- **A player's card everywhere:** tap another player's name tag in any world: REPORT (what happened: mean words,
  scary, asked for secrets, something else) and BLOCK work for anyone, friend or not. The host can REMOVE a player from
  their world; that player can't come back.
- **Photo sharing is a grown-up's choice:** in GROWN-UPS → PLAY & PHOTOS; it starts off for children under 13, and
  photos are always saved on the device.
- **Stronger child protection online:** a phone that learns a player is under 13 always makes their account stricter,
  never less strict; chat and rejoining end when a friendship ends; the host knows its guests' names and ages from the
  server, not from their games; notifications are deleted after 30 days; the grown-up question rests a minute after
  three wrong answers; a privacy page in the grown-up area.

**Fixes** (1.0.1 adds: Back during a loading screen shows the pause menu; Back in MOVE BUTTONS closes only the button
editor; a player the host removed on a home network can't join straight back; Pip's card follows BIG text; LOW keeps
summoned monsters to its limit)
- The Bubble Cannon traps a small monster in its bubble first, then the hit lands when it pops; new worlds no longer
  make wrong caves when a lot of ground loads at once; village roofs no longer scare monsters away like lanterns; a
  buddy whose child left waits instead of following someone else; "called an owl", "1 block", "1 star"; a chest mimic
  can always be woken again; SETTINGS shows which page is open; the game is English only.

**Play notes (for the Play Console's "What's new", under 500 characters):**
Pip teaches you to play! Bigger buttons, a left-hand layout, movable buttons, bigger text and sound captions. The game
is half the size and smoother on small phones. Weapons are as strong as they are rare. Summon any creature in the
Sandbox. Tap any player's name to report, block or (as host) remove them.
