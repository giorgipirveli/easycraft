# EasyCraft 1.0.2 — what's new (2026-09-28)
Since 1.0.1. The same game now also plays in any browser (https://178-104-20-64.sslip.io/play/); this release brings the
phone app up to it.

**New for players**
- **School with Professor Hoot (#68):** SCHOOL on the main menu. Pick your grade (4 to 8), then lessons read aloud and
  short rounds of practice (tap the answer, type a number, put things in order, build the answer with blocks), with
  hints, stars and gold for each unit you master. All of grades 4 to 8 is here: maths, science and English, 201 units,
  with endless new practice questions in maths. Grown-ups can see the progress and lock the grade (GROWN-UPS in the
  School).
- **Fly to the Moon and the planets (#55):** fly high enough above any world and space opens: "Where shall we land?"
  The Moon (float when you jump; a Moon Base to find; moon bunnies), Planet Boing (everything bounces) and the
  Rainbow Planet (a rainbow arch to climb). A golden portal, or flying up again to HOME, brings you back.
- **Double tap to fly (#54):** in the Sandbox, two quick taps on JUMP lift you up and start flying; tapping on keeps
  the jumps and the parachute.
- **Your account on every device (#67):** your name, look, levels, badges, universes, crystals, Secret Book finds,
  pets and settings are kept with your online account and come back on a new phone or in a browser. A grown-up makes a
  sign-in key: GROWN-UPS → ONLINE PLAY → OTHER DEVICES → MAKE A KEY; on the other device: PLAY ONLINE → I HAVE AN
  ACCOUNT. Your gold always follows the server.
- **No age question, no grown-up checks (#69):** PLAY goes straight to your name; nothing is limited by age.
- **"Made by Alexander Pirveli"** under the name (#56).
- **SIMPLE or CLASSIC controls any time (#66):** on the first page of SETTINGS, beside the style, in the menu and in
  the pause menu.

**Fixes**
- SETTINGS in the main menu fits the screen again: BACK was below the bottom on some phones.
- Smoother in the browser: no more long freeze when a world first appears (the game prepares its pictures on the
  menu and the loading card).
- Smoother: the block palette and the Secret Book are built when you first open them, a new world's Theater site is
  found in the background, animals wake up area by area (fewer pauses when a world loads or you arrive somewhere new).
