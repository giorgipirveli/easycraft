# EasyCraft 1.0.3 — what's new (2026-09-29)
Since 1.0.2 (built 2026-09-29, never uploaded: 1.0.3 replaces it and includes everything in
`docs/playtests/whats-new-1.0.2.md`). The phone app catches up with the browser version.

**New for players**
- **The School grows to every subject (#70, #73):** grades 3 to 8 and twelve subjects (maths, science, English,
  history, geography, citizenship, computing, art, music, health and PE, design and technology, projects), following
  the world's leading school programmes. Grade 3 is written in all twelve, grade 4 in maths, science, English,
  history, geography, citizenship, computing, art and music; more every day. New kinds of practice: sort things into
  groups, match pairs, code a robot to the flag, copy a rhythm, mix paint colours, and LISTEN to tunes and claps.
- **The Star Map (#72, #74):** fly into space and see our solar system in its real order, with its moons, and every
  universe as its own planet. Tap any of them for three real facts, read aloud; land where you can. The Space Book
  (a new tab in the book) keeps every planet you looked at and every one you landed on.
- **Mars and Europa, real worlds (#74):** land on Mars (red dust, a six-wheeled rover, a giant volcano, a great
  canyon, ice caps, two little moons; you are lighter there) and on Europa (white ice under Jupiter, reddish cracks,
  geysers that throw you up high, and a way down to the ocean under the ice). Every find teaches a real fact.
- **The Science Lab (#74):** build a lab bench (or find one in a village) and do real experiments: float or sink,
  magnetic or not, melting and freezing, mixing paint and light, what plants need, a simple circuit. Guess, try it,
  then find out why; the Lab Book keeps your results.
- **Jump taps fly and go up (#75):** tap JUMP quickly to take off, and every tap while flying lifts you higher.

**Fixes**
- Planet Boing and the Rainbow Planet keep their own skies in the realistic style; the Rainbow Planet shows its
  rainbow on every terrace.
- Exercises with long answers always fit the screen.

EasyCraft 1.0.3: the School now covers every subject from grade 3 (twelve subjects, new kinds of practice), a Star Map
