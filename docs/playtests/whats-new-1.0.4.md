# EasyCraft 1.0.4 — what's new (2026-10-01)
Since 1.0.3 (built 2026-09-29, never uploaded: 1.0.4 replaces it and includes everything in
`docs/playtests/whats-new-1.0.3.md`). The phone app catches up with the browser version again.

**New for players**
- **The School, grades 3 to 6 complete, and grade 7 (#70, #73):** every subject of grades 3-6 (546 units) and grade 7's
  maths, science, English, history, geography, citizenship, computing, art and music; every lesson rewritten to one
  quality bar (#94): shorter sentences, a picture where it teaches, moving pictures (the water cycle, the Moon going
  round, orbits, pendulums, a rocket's stages), more hands-on practice (sort, match, order, numbers), harder steps at the
  higher levels, and the worked answer after a right second try.
- **EasyCraft Shorts in the TV room (#94, #96):** short learning animations drawn by the game, swiped up and down like
  short videos: how to build a rocket, lift-off and staging, the planets going round the Sun, the Moon's phases, Saturn's
  rings, a home on the Moon, Venus's city in the clouds, the Seven Wonders, and a "Lesson in a minute" for every School
  unit. Tap the heart to keep one; LEARN IT opens what it teaches. Plays without the internet.
- **Rockets and missions (#78, #79):** build a rocket from real parts on the Launch Site, check it like real engineers,
  buy parts with the gold you earn by learning, sit at the controls and fly it to the Moon; **friends fly together** as
  one crew and land together.
- **The cosmos (#72, #74):** new worlds to land on: Mercury, Titan, Io, Venus's floating city, Saturn's rings, Pluto and
  Triton, each with its real facts; colonies under domes on the Moon and Mars; mining the Moon's and Mars's riches (ice
  into water, air and rocket fuel); robots and aliens to meet; the Star Map's planets move round the Sun as they really do.
- **Wonders of the World (#78):** the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, the New Seven Wonders and the Seven Natural
  Wonders to visit, each with its story, a stamp and a quiz.
- **More alive (#94):** birds by day and fireflies at night, bees and butterflies in the meadows, characters who react and
  wave, new emotes (Think, Yawn, Stretch), menus that move, gold that flies to its counter.
- **A better look:** far hills melt into the sky, a golden morning sun, orange sunsets, sparkling water.
- **Play alone without the internet (#93, #95):** everything you do alone works offline and is saved to your account
  when the internet comes back ("Back online!").
- **Invite friends (#79, #81):** an invite code and link; gold for you and your friend when they start learning.

**Fixes**
- Gold never counts twice when you sign in on a new device with your key.
- Online play stays steady when a friend joins (#92).

EasyCraft 1.0.4: the School covers grades 3-6 in every subject and much of grade 7, every lesson rewritten to be clearer;
EasyCraft Shorts, short learning animations to swipe in the TV room; build and fly your own rocket, with friends; new
worlds from Mercury to Pluto; the world's wonders; and play alone without the internet.
