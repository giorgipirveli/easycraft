# EasyCraft 1.0.5 — what's new (2026-10-16)
Since 1.0.4 (built 2026-10-01, never uploaded: 1.0.5 replaces it and includes everything in
`docs/playtests/whats-new-1.0.4.md`). The phone app catches up with the browser version again.

**New for players**
- **Your password, and LOG IN anywhere (#105):** you make a password with your name when you start (players from before
  are asked once). On another phone or computer, LOG IN with your name and password and your game is there: your name,
  progress, looks and gold. One device at a time: the old one is logged out and says "This device was logged out. LOG IN
  to play here."
- **LOG OUT, with no grown-ups' step (#104):** on the main menu, in SETTINGS and in PLAY ONLINE. Everything is saved to
  your account first, and your worlds stay on the device.
- **A real Moon landing (#106):** the last part of every landing is yours to fly: watch the height, speed and fuel dials
  and land gently. A crash brings you home by parachute with half your hearts, and some of the rocket's parts break:
  rebuild them and try again.
- **The rocket made easier (#98):** Mission Control has a ready plan for every mission; BUILD IT FOR ME builds it layer by
  layer, the parts are bought in one place, and prices are halved (the Moon: 450 gold).
- **Ganymede:** a new world to land on, Jupiter's biggest moon, with its real facts.
- **MAKE MY SHORT:** make your own Short (how I built it, 3 facts about…, step by step) with your photos, stamps and
  music; it stays on your device.

**Fixes**
- DELETE MY ACCOUNT really deletes the account; nothing makes it again by itself (#104).
- Gold is exact online: nothing is lost or counted twice when a friend plays in your world.

EasyCraft 1.0.5: make a password and LOG IN on any device; LOG OUT in one tap; fly the Moon landing yourself; an easier
rocket with Mission Control's plans; Ganymede; and make your own Shorts.
