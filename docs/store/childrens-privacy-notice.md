# "Your stuff is yours" — the children's privacy notice (DRAFT)

For children to read (or hear). Planned to be shown in the game before the first online game (next to the "Stay
safe online" card) and in the settings, read by the game's read-aloud (M3 Task 21a), one picture per line (M5 art
pass). **Not in the game yet** (finding F3 in `pre-launch-checklist.md`: the grown-up area now links the privacy
policy; this card with its pictures is M5 work). Short words for early readers; English only (owner #52,
2026-09-25). Legal review with the privacy policy. Updated on 2026-09-25 (M5 Task 4: races, trades, codes, photos,
read-aloud, the player card).

| Picture | English |
|---|---|
| 🏠 a house with a lock | Your worlds and builds stay on your tablet or phone. Online, your name keeps your progress safe. |
| 📷 a camera | Your photos go to your phone's gallery. A grown-up decides if you can share them. |
| 🔊 a speaker | Read-aloud uses your phone's own voice. |
| 🎁 a present | A build you send to a friend goes only to your friend. |
| 🏁 a race flag | Your race on your course goes only to your friends. |
| 🤝 two hands | You trade only when you both say READY. Nothing is kept. |
| 🔑 a key | A secret code is checked, then forgotten. |
| 🙈 a hidden face | We never ask for your real name, where you live or your photo. |
| 🏷️ a name tag | To play online you pick a made-up name. Never use your real name. |
| 👨‍👩‍👧 a grown-up | If you are under 13, a grown-up must say yes before you play online. |
| 💬 a speech bubble with a picture | You talk to friends with picture phrases. Nobody keeps them. |
| 🚩 a flag | If someone is mean, tap them and choose REPORT or BLOCK. A person will look at every report. |
| 🗑️ a bin | A grown-up can delete your online account any time. |
| 🚫📺 no ads | No ads. No tricks. You never have to pay to play. |

Notes for the art pass: the emoji above only name the picture to draw (the game draws its pictures in code, like
`OnlineIcon`); the card shows at most seven lines at a time (two pages of seven), in the order above. "READY",
"REPORT" and "BLOCK" are the game's own button words (the trade window; the player card on every name tag, spec
§17.3); keep them the same if the buttons change.

A "secret code is forgotten" means: the text is never stored; the server keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of a
code that worked (`privacy-policy.md`). The lawyer may prefer "checked, not kept".

"Your name keeps your progress safe" (owner #67, 2026-09-28): an online account's progress backup (never the worlds)
comes back on a new phone or browser when a grown-up signs it in with the account's sign-in key. The key is a
grown-up's thing (made and typed behind the parent gate), so this card does not mention it; the privacy policy does.
