# Delete your EasyCraft online account

EasyCraft keeps nothing about you unless you play **online**. To delete an online account and everything our
server keeps for it (the online name, age group, friends, the game backup, gold history, used secret codes, the
sign-in key's and the password's fingerprints and every device signed in to the account, shared builds and races,
and private messages):

1. **In the game** (the fastest way): SETTINGS → GROWN-UPS (a grown-up solves the parent gate) → ONLINE PLAY →
   **DELETE MY ONLINE ACCOUNT**, type the account's password, and hold the red button. The server deletes the account
   at once, and the phone forgets the friends' presents and races it kept, and this player too (the name, progress,
   gold, looks and books; the worlds on the phone stay): the game starts again for a new player.
2. **By e-mail**: write to **parkingi-support@googlegroups.com** from any address with the subject "Delete my
   EasyCraft account". Give the online name and, if you can, the date it was last used. We never ask for the
   password by e-mail.

We delete the account and its data within **30 days**, and it is gone from our backups within **35 days**.
Reports other players made about the account are kept up to 12 months after they were reviewed, with the name
taken off. Worlds, builds, photos and the Memory Book on your own device are not on our server: deleting the app
removes them (photos you took stay in your phone's gallery until you delete them there).

## Deleting only some data
You can keep the account and delete parts of it:
- **In the game:** delete a present or a friend's stamp (PRESENTS, or the palette's GIFTS page), delete a sticker a
  friend left on your showcase, or take your showcase down (the races on it go with it). A grown-up can switch
  online play or chat off in SETTINGS → GROWN-UPS, and in ONLINE PLAY → OTHER DEVICES make a new sign-in key (the old
  one stops working) or sign every other device out (a lost phone).
- **By e-mail** to the address above, with the online name and what should go (for example "the game backup" or
  "the gold history"). We answer within 30 days.
