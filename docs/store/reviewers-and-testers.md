# Reviewers and testers you can trust (owner, 2026-09-24: "find some public reviewers who we can invite and ask them reviews")

Checked by the lead on 2026-09-25. Everything here is free unless it says otherwise.

## 1. The 12 testers for 14 days (needed before production)
Google's rule for personal developer accounts created after 13 Nov 2023: at least **12 testers opted in to a
closed test continuously for 14 days**. Then Play Console → Dashboard → **Apply for production**, which asks
how you recruited testers, whether they used the features, what feedback they gave, and what you changed.
([Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465))
- **Real people on real phones only.** Testers need their own Google accounts on real Android devices. Emulators
  and made-up accounts don't count and put the whole developer account at risk (it also holds Parkingi).
- **How a tester joins:** they are on the list ("EasyCraft testers" in Play Console, or a Google Group), they
  open the join link while signed in to that Google account, tap **Become a tester**, and install EasyCraft
  from Google Play. They stay in for 14 days.
- **Where to find them:** family, friends, classmates' parents, colleagues. A **Google Group** is easiest: you
  send one link, people join the group, and they become testers.
- **Tester exchanges** (other developers test your app while you test theirs; for example the subreddit
  r/AndroidClosedTesting, or the "Testers Community" site and app): these are real people, but judge each one
  yourself. **Never pay for "12 testers" from a service that uses emulators or made-up accounts.**
- **Private feedback:** Play Console → Monitor and improve → Ratings and reviews → **Testing feedback** shows
  what testers write on the game's Play page. Their comments help the production application.

## 2. Trusted reviews for a kids' game
| Who | What it is | How | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Play Expert Approved** (formerly Teacher Approved) | Teachers and children's media specialists rate kids' apps for quality and age-appropriateness. Approved apps get a badge and can be featured on the Play **Kids** tab. ([Google Play](https://google.play/business/programs/teacherapproved/), [Families policies](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335)) | Play Console → Monitor and improve → Policy and programmes → **Expert approved** (or Target audience → step 4). We chose "not now" on 2026-09-24. Opt in once EasyCraft is in production and polished (M5). | Free |
| **Common Sense Media** | The best-known independent reviews of kids' media for parents. Creators and funders can't influence the ratings. ([how they rate](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/about-us/our-mission/about-our-ratings/apps)) | There's no paid submission. Once the game is public, suggest it through their contact page; they choose what to review. | Free (no guarantee) |
| **Parents on the Play page** | Real ratings and reviews once the game is public. | Nudging for ratings is allowed only in neutral ways: no rewards for reviews, and no asking children. A grown-up area prompt after several good sessions is fine. | Free |
| **kidSAFE Seal** / **PRIVO** (COPPA safe-harbor programs) | Independent checks of children's privacy practices, with a seal parents recognise. | Apply on their sites after the lawyer's review of the privacy policy. | Paid |

## 3. What we won't do
No paid fake reviews, no review swaps that promise stars, no rewards in the game for reviews (Play policy),
and no testers who don't really play.
