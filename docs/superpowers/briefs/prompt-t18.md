## Build (TDD for every rule; pure code in `core/mind/`)
1. **TinyNet** (`core/mind/tiny_net.gd`): one hidden layer MLP on `PackedFloat32Array` (inputs ~16, hidden ~12, outputs = the species' actions), forward pass, an output-layer reward update (REINFORCE-lite / contextual bandit: small step, weights clamped), deterministic init from a seed, to/from save (base64 or arrays; ≤ 2 KB per saved mind). Measure: 10,000 decisions under 200 ms on this desktop (write the number in your notes; the PC is loaded by other agents — record the load average).
2. **Mind** (`core/mind/mind.gd`): senses → drives (hunger, sleepiness, fear, curiosity, company; monsters: boldness, caution; bots: helpfulness, play) → memory (≤ 8 players by name with like/fear and last seen; ≤ 8 places: food, den/bed, danger; forgetting over time) → the net's action scores tilted by drives and a per-individual personality offset (from its id) → a gentle softmax pick. `reward(amount)` after an action's outcome. Species tables (`core/mind/species/*.gd` or data): default weights hand-made so a fresh creature already behaves sensibly, the action list, the reward table.
3. **Animals** (part 1): real-animal behaviour on the mind — graze/wander by day, sleep together at night near trees or their den, herds follow a leader, curiosity about new blocks/players, fear memory (keeps away from a player who hit it, for a while), affection memory (runs over to a player who fed/petted it; pets remember their owner by name), favourite places. Keep M1b's rules (active only near players, 10 Hz decisions, shared players list, no per-event materials) and all existing animal tests green.
4. **Bot friends** (part 1): learn the child's style — builders pick the child's favourite blocks and shapes (from the blocks the child places; `WorldService.block_changed`), keep builds the child keeps (+) and avoid designs the child breaks (−); explorers learn whether the child follows ores, animals or secrets (the child mines/tames/opens what was pointed at → +); follow distance adapts (the child keeps walking away → more space); fighters guard the side monsters come from.
