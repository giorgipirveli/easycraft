## Global constraints
- English only (owner #52); British spelling in the game's words (§22.6 ruling 3); spelling exercises never test a word
  spelled differently in the US and the UK.
- Fair fun (§6): no timers, countdowns, streaks, "come back" lines, random rewards; rewards fixed and once; nothing is
  lost or expires; praise effort and strategy, never talent; no "failed", "wrong!" buzzers or red crosses.
- Every screen fits 1280 × 720 and 20:9 (2400 × 1080 window = a 1600 × 720 canvas); buttons at least 135 × 64 px.
- Every lesson card and exercise can be read aloud (`ReadAloud`); "Read to me" reads prompts by itself.
- Our own words only; no other game's names (`test_identity_words.gd` scans `data/`); no guilt words
  (`test_no_guilt_words.gd`'s list, checked on the data by the content test too).
- The default font has × ÷ ² ³ ° ≤ ≥ √ ½ ¼ ¾ − π but no stars, arrows or ticks: draw those.
- Metric units, time and gold; the game's characters and places in stories.
- Never touch the profile sync (`net/`, `server/`, `core/save/profile_backup.gd`): the lead or the sync agent adds
  `out.school.merge_from(other.school)` to `ProfileBackup.merge`.
- The menu loads `res://ui/school/school_screen.gd` only when SCHOOL is pressed (startup unchanged, §15.3).

## The data (exercise schema)
```json
{"type": "choice", "level": 1, "prompt": "…", "picture": {"kind": "array", "rows": 3, "cols": 4},
 "choices": ["12", "7", "34", "43"], "answer": 0, "hint": "…", "explain": "…"}
{"type": "number", "prompt": "3 km = ? m", "answer": "3000", "hint": "…", "explain": "…"}
{"type": "truefalse", "prompt": "…", "answer": false, "hint": "…", "explain": "…"}
{"type": "order", "prompt": "Smallest first", "items": ["4,210", "4,021", "4,201"], "answer": [1, 2, 0], …}
{"type": "build", "prompt": "Build 3 rows of 4 blocks.", "board": {"cols": 8, "rows": 5, "block": 9, "given": [], "view": "wall"},
 "goal": {"kind": "array", "rows": 3, "cols": 4, "any_turn": true}, …}
```
A number answer is compared by exact value (`5/8` = `10/16`, `0.5` = `0.50`); the pad shows `.` `/` `-` only when the
answer has one. Board goals: `count` {n}, `array` {rows, cols, any_turn}, `rectangle` {area, perimeter?, min_side?},
`fraction` {num, den} (of the board's cells), `mirror` {sum} (cell x mirrors to sum − x). A unit's `generators` are
`{"gen": "g4m.round_to", "level": 2}`; a generator returns an exercise of the same schema.
