# Space: the Moon and the planets (owner feedback #55) — plan

> For an agentic worker in its own worktree: no editor, emulator, adb, browser or exports (the lead verifies). Spec §21.
> Strict TDD (GUT, headless); one Godot at a time under `nice`; the full suite through the PC-wide slots.

**Goal:** flying high enough above any world opens "Where shall we land?"; the Moon, Planet Boing and Rainbow Planet are
new worlds with their own ground, sky, gravity, light, sounds, secrets, creatures and a landmark; a child comes home
through the portal by the landing pad or by flying up again.

**How it plugs in (no parallel system):** a space world is a **universe descriptor** (`world/universes/moon.gd`, …) that
`UniverseRegistry` loads by number (16-18) from its own `SPACE` table next to the `LADDER`: the same `Game.universe`,
`ModeRules.slot`, `WorldService.setup(…, universe)`, `SkyCycle.palette`, `Music.use_tracks`, animals, secrets
(`SecretDirector` + the descriptor's `secret_rules`), loading card and picture cards (`UniverseArt`). Travel reuses the
sandbox's `_travel` / `_travel_sandbox` shape and the owner #39 portal home (`ReturnGate`, `arrival_gate`).
