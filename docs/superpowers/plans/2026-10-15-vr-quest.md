# VR on the Meta Quest — the plan (owner #101, 2026-10-15: priority 1)

> The owner: "we have to start in parallel working on VR mode, I have meta quest and we have to adapt the game for VR, this is
> now N1 priority in our plan when this is done we continue other remaining works."

**Goal:** EasyCraft playable in a Meta Quest: stand in your own world, look round, walk, build and break, use every menu, the
School and the boards, fly the rocket, play with friends: comfortable for children and at the headset's refresh rate.

**The research:** `docs/devlog/parts/vr-research.md` (the platform; the adaptation map with files and lines; comfort; the
budget; phases; the two-agent split; open questions with defaults). Spec §3b "VR on the Meta Quest" points here.

## Rulings (the lead's; cost if wrong in brackets)
1. **Two routes, one code.** One XR rig (XROrigin3D, XRCamera3D, two XRController3D) and world-space panels, started by
   `Vr` from either interface: **WebXR** in the Quest's browser (a separate web export, feature tag `webxr`, served at
   `/vr/`, beside `/play/` and outside its service worker's scope, so the flat web build stays exactly as it is) and **OpenXR** in a native Quest APK (preset "Quest", feature
   tag `quest`, Gradle, arm64, min SDK 32, target SDK 34, package `com.giorgi.easycraft.quest`, Mobile renderer; a "Quest GL"
   twin with `--rendering-method gl_compatibility` for the A/B on the headset). The WebXR link reaches the owner first (no
   developer account, no cable). [A route that doesn't work on the device: the other carries on with the same code.]
2. **Quest 2 is the floor** (XR2 Gen 1, 72 Hz, about 100 draws, 750K triangles); `vr_plus` for Quest 3 later. The `vr`
   graphics preset starts from LOW: no shadows, view 64, blocky style only on Quest 2, no glow, simple water, sway off, half
   particles, caps of 6 monsters and 8 animals near, foveation high and dynamic. [Looks plainer than the phone on Quest 3
   until vr_plus.]
3. **Comfort first (children):** first person only; smooth stick walking with a light vignette, snap turn 45° (smooth turn
   opt-in), teleport on right stick up (P3); eye at the avatar's height ("game height"; "real height" an option); never a
   camera move the child did not make: no shake, no knockback on the VR body, no orbiting menu, every jump in place a black
   fade (0.15 s out, 0.25 s in); a 30-minute break card. [A child feels sick: switch the default to teleport-first.]
4. **Age:** Meta's Quest accounts are 10+ (parent-managed 10-12); the game itself adds no age gate (owner #69); the VR
   store listing waits for the owner and the lawyer (like Play's). No Meta Platform SDK in version 1 (no Meta purchases).
5. **Downloads need the owner's yes** (the session's safety rule): the Godot OpenXR Vendors plugin 5.1.0 (GitHub, about
   48 MB, MIT + the Oculus SDK licence for its Meta part: the Meta manifest extras, Quest features, Space Warp) and Monado
   (Ubuntu's OpenXR runtime, for a desktop headset simulator). Until then: WebXR needs nothing new; the APK uses the Khronos
   loader Godot's own Gradle build adds.
6. **Tests:** pure rules and scenes with fake trackers under GUT (`Vr.simulate`), the desktop suite always green with VR off
   (off VR every `Vr` call is a cheap no-op); the emulator for non-VR regressions; the owner's Quest for VR acceptance (adb
   with the Quest's own serial on port 5038, never emulator-5556). [Bugs only the headset shows: the owner's time is the
   bottleneck; the ALPHA channel later.]
7. **The TV (YouTube in an Android WebView)** does not show in immersive VR: a sign "The TV plays on a phone or tablet";
   EasyCraft Shorts play.

## The contract (lead, committed before the agents start)
- `autoloads/vr.gd` (autoload **Vr**, `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`): `active() -> bool`, `camera` (XRCamera3D or null),
  `hand_ray(side: StringName) -> Array` ([origin, direction] in world space; `&"right"` by default, the left-handed setting
  swaps), `anchor(node: Node3D, kind: StringName)` (the origin follows a node: `&"feet"`, `&"seat"`, `&"menu"`),
  `fade_out(seconds) / fade_in(seconds)` (awaitable; instant off VR), `ui_viewport() -> SubViewport` (null off VR),
  `ui_wants_pointer() -> bool`, `start(kind: StringName) -> String` (`&"openxr"` / `&"webxr"`; "" or why not), signals
  `started`, `ended`, `recentered`, `focus_changed(focused: bool)`; `simulate(on: bool)` for tests (a fake head and hands).
- `core/vr/vr_rules.gd` (`VrRules`, pure): the settings keys and defaults, the snap angle, the fade times.
- `core/vr/ui_layers.gd` (`UiLayers.route(layer, owner)`): off VR, `owner.add_child(layer)`; in VR, the layer goes into the
  VR canvas. The seams: `scenes/sandbox.gd` (the HUD layer through `UiLayers.route`; the crosshair `or Vr.active()`).

## The work, two agents in parallel (fixed files: `docs/devlog/parts/vr-research.md` "Two agents in parallel")
- **VR-RIG (the world side):** the body of `Vr`, `entities/vr/*` (rig, input, comfort, teleport, hands), the action map,
  `VrRules`, the player and camera, the aim seam (24 call sites → `Player.aim_ray`), Feel in VR, `GraphicsPreset`'s `vr`,
  the flights (P3). Tests `test_vr_rig_*.gd`, notes `docs/devlog/parts/vr-rig.md`.
- **VR-UI (the panel side):** `UiLayers`, `entities/vr/vr_panel.gd`, `vr_pointer.gd`, `ui/vr/*` (keyboard, VR COMFORT page,
  controller card, TV sign, break card), the menus, settings, pause, loading card, PadFocus, the HUD in VR, the readability
  pass, the ENTER VR button and the WebXR start. Tests `test_vr_ui_*.gd`, notes `docs/devlog/parts/vr-ui.md`.
- **The lead:** `project.godot`, `export_presets.cfg` (the Quest presets, the Web VR preset), `scenes/sandbox.gd`,
  `scenes/main.gd`, docs, merges, the builds, the devices.

## Phases (details and acceptance in the research)
- **P0 (lead + both agents' first commits):** the contract; the presets; the dev loop without a headset; the first APK and
  the `/vr/` link for the owner.
- **P1:** stand in your world, look, walk, snap turn, break and place with the laser, the main menu and the pause menu as
  panels, fades, Feel off, the `vr` preset, a small VR COMFORT page. Acceptance on the owner's Quest: menu → SANDBOX, walk
  100 m, break and place 10, pause, SAVE & QUIT; ≥ 72 FPS average in Green Meadows, no frame over 28 ms after 5 s, ≤ 150
  draws. The desktop suite green.
- **P2:** every screen readable and usable (School, boards, shop, books, maps, Shorts, chat), a VR keyboard, the wrist HUD.
- **P3:** the rocket flight seated in the capsule, rides and gadgets comfortable, teleport, photo as a held camera, Size
  Magic through world scale.
- **P4:** hands and multiplayer bodies (head and hands sent, cross-play with phones and the web).
- **P5:** 72 Hz in every world (`tools/vr_bench.gd`), then `vr_plus` for Quest 3.

## What the owner does (once)
- Say which Quest it is (Settings → About), or plug it in and we read it.
- For the WebXR link: open `https://178-104-20-64.sslip.io/vr/` in the Quest's browser, press ENTER VR, allow it.
- For the APK: a Meta developer account (18+, verified), Developer Mode on in the Meta Horizon phone app, the headset on this
  PC by USB-C with "Allow USB debugging" → "Always allow".
- Yes or no to the two downloads in ruling 5.
