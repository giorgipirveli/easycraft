# VR round 4: hands that hold things, and wonders to hold (owner #110) — the plan and the frozen contract

The owner (2026-10-16 11:24, #110): "consider in vr mode we should have hands and be able to hendle items using vr
joysticks, research what can be done else, wjat exciting things with vr". The research:
`docs/devlog/parts/vr-ideas-research.md` (the best games' hands, the Quest 3's features, what children love, safety; 12
rulings; the top 8). This plan adopts its §6 and §7 as the lead's rulings, with Life (#112, #115) in mind: the shared
grab is what Life's Experiences will hold coins, cloths and extinguishers with.

## The frozen contract (the lead commits it before the agents start; names never change after)
- **`VrGrabbable`** (`entities/vr/vr_grabbable.gd`, a Node3D component added as a child of anything a hand can take):
  - `@export var kind: StringName` = `&"hold"` (one hand), `&"two_hand"` (both hands: scale or aim, the planet, the bow),
    `&"tool"` (held and used with the trigger: the lens, a mallet, the extinguisher);
  - `@export var throwable := true`, `@export var far_pull := true` (the laser's three-stage pull), `@export var
    hold_offset := Transform3D.IDENTITY` (how it sits in the hand);
  - signals `hovered(side: StringName, on: bool)`, `grabbed(side: StringName)`, `released(side: StringName, velocity:
    Vector3)`, `used(side: StringName, amount: float)` (the trigger while held, 0..1);
  - `func held_by() -> StringName` (`&""`, `&"left"`, `&"right"`, `&"both"`).
- **`VrGrabRules`** (`core/vr/vr_grab_rules.gd`, pure, class_name `VrGrabRules`): `NEAR_REACH := 0.12` (m from the hand),
  `FAR_RANGE := 4.0`, `PULL_HOLD := 0.6` (s: a hold pulls for children who can't flick), `THROW_WINDOW := 0.1` (s),
  `THROW_SCALE := 1.5`, `THROW_MAX := 12.0` (m/s), `BUBBLE := 0.5` (m round the head), and
  `static func throw_velocity(samples: Array) -> Vector3` (the peak of the last THROW_WINDOW × THROW_SCALE, capped).
- **`Vr.haptic(side, amplitude, seconds, frequency := 0.0)`**: the existing call gains an optional frequency (0: the
  runtime's default); every existing caller is unchanged.
- **The grip (ruling R1):** it grabs when a `VrGrabbable` is within NEAR_REACH or under the laser; otherwise it steps the
  hotbar as today.
- The rulings R1-R12 of the research §7 stand as written (kinematic hands that stop at a block's face, never pushing the
  body; zoom only inside a lens; the 0.5 m personal bubble; no open voice chat; no Passthrough Camera API; mixed reality
  in round 5; arm-powered movement opt-in; our own instrument tones).
