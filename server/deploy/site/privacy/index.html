EasyCraft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · Who we are: EasyCraft is made by Giorgi Pirveli (API Studio), Tbilisi, Georgia ("we"). Contact: parkingi-support@googlegroups.com — we answer within 30 days.

The short version

  • You can play EasyCraft without giving us anything: Sandbox, Survivor, playing with bot friends and playing with family over home Wi-Fi keep everything on your device.
  • Online play (with friends over the internet) needs a small account on our server: an online name you choose, your age group, and your game progress. For children under 13, online play stays off until a parent agrees.
  • No ads. No tracking. No selling or renting of data, ever. No voice chat. No AI chatbots: bot friends understand what you say inside the game itself.
  • Purchases (when the shop is on): only a grown-up can buy EasyCraft Plus or an outfit pack, in the grown-up area behind a parent gate. Google Play takes the payment; we never see card or bank details. We keep only which products the account owns (in the game's profile, and in its online backup) so they can be restored on a new phone. Today the shop is not open.

What stays on your device

  • Your worlds, builds and stamps, settings, gold, unlocks and progress, the age group you picked (never your birth date), and how long the game was played today (for the grown-up area).
  • Photos you take in the game go to your phone's gallery, in an album called "EasyCraft". They leave the phone only if you share one yourself with Android's share button (you pick the app each time), or if your phone's own settings back up its gallery. We never receive them. For a player under 13 the share button is off until a parent turns photo sharing on in the game's grown-up area (PLAY & PHOTOS, behind the parent check); a parent can switch it off for any player.
  • The Memory Book (your game's special moments and the photos that go with them) is kept only on this device.
  • Boy, girl or just me (the first choice of your look) stays on this device; we never learn it. Other players see only your avatar.
  • Read-aloud: when the game reads words out loud, it uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice. The words go to that voice on your phone, never to us.
  • Bot friends: what you say to your bot friends is understood by the game on the phone that runs the world. There is no online AI service.
  • Presents your friends sent you are kept on your device until you delete them. Your friends' races (their ghosts) are kept on your device so you can race them offline, until you delete the online account (or report that friend).

Deleting the app deletes all of this, except the photos in your gallery (they are your own pictures).

Worlds stay on the device where they were made, also when you play online: a world's blocks, what you built, the Survivor bag in it and its buddies and pets are too big to copy to our server. What the online backup brings to a new phone is listed below.

Playing in a web browser (the game's web page, installed or not): all of the above stays in that browser's own storage on your device, for the game's page only. The page loads nothing from other websites and sets no cookies: no ads, no analytics. Photos are offered as a download instead of going to a gallery. Clearing the browser's data for the game (or uninstalling the installed web app) deletes it; the online backup, if you play online, can bring your progress back: a grown-up signs the browser in again with the account's sign-in key (below).

A "place" in the game (where your player stands, a quest's place, a ghost's path) is a spot in a game world, never your real location; we never ask for your location.

Children under 13 (and under the age of digital consent where you live)

  • Online play for children under 13 is not open yet. Before it opens, we will name here the parental consent service we use; then, before a child under 13 can add friends, be found by name, be invited, chat, trade online or share builds and races, a parent must give verifiable consent through it. The parent's e-mail address goes to that service to contact the parent; we don't keep it.
  • In every world, a child can report or block any player from the player's name tag, and the player whose world it is can remove anyone from it.
  • Even with consent: friend requests wait for a grown-up's OK, chat is picture phrases only (nothing typed, nothing stored), a child trades online only with their own friends, other players see a child's name on a shared race only with consent, and online play can be switched off at any time in the grown-up area.
  • Parents can see and change these settings behind the parent gate in the game (SETTINGS → GROWN-UPS), and can ask us at parkingi-support@googlegroups.com to see or delete their child's data, or to stop collecting it.
  • The game can make an account's age group stricter (for example when a phone learns the player is under 13), but it never moves an account from under 13 to 13 and over. If the age group was picked wrong, or a child has turned 13, a parent writes to us and we change it.

Who else sees data

  • Other players see your online name, your avatar and what you do in a world you share, the messages you send them, what you offer them in a trade, and (only your friends) the builds and races you share with them.
  • Service providers who work only on our instructions: our server host Hetzner Online GmbH (Nuremberg, Germany) and, once under-13 online play opens, the parental consent service named above. They may not use the data for anything else.
  • Nobody else. We don't sell or share data for advertising. We would disclose data only if the law requires it.
  • (When the Shorts Theater is switched on; it is off today:) videos play in YouTube's and TikTok's own embedded players, under their privacy policies; children under 13 see only videos made for kids, with tracking off, and never TikTok.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your online account: SETTINGS → GROWN-UPS → ONLINE PLAY → DELETE MY ONLINE ACCOUNT in the game, or e-mail parkingi-support@googlegroups.com (how: https://178-104-20-64.sslip.io/delete-account). We delete the account and everything the table above lists within 30 days (backups within 35 days).
  • Delete only some of it: delete a present, a sticker or a friend's stamp in the game, or take your showcase down (its races go with it); or e-mail us about anything else.
  • A lost phone or a key given away: GROWN-UPS → ONLINE PLAY → OTHER DEVICES: MAKE A NEW KEY stops the old key, and SIGN OUT OTHER DEVICES stops every other device from signing in to the account.
  • See, correct or get a copy of your data; object to or limit its use; withdraw a parent's consent; complain to your data protection authority (in Georgia: the Personal Data Protection Service; in the EU/UK: your local authority).
  • Legal bases (EU/UK): running the game you asked for (contract); a parent's consent for children under the age of digital consent; keeping the service secure (legitimate interests, for logs and reports).

Security

Connections to our server are encrypted (HTTPS/WSS). The server stores data in a database that is not reachable from the internet, with daily backups. Only the owner can open the admin console, with a strong password. We follow a written data-retention and security policy.

Where data is stored

On a server in Germany (European Union), run by Hetzner Online GmbH. If that is outside your country, we protect the transfer as the data protection law that applies to you requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell players in the game, and ask parents again where the law requires it, before the change applies.
